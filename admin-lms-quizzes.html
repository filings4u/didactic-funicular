Quiz Flow

Quizzes are knowledge checks that occur after each module. They are separate from the final assessment required before certificate completion.

1Learner completes moduleAll required lesson content is completed.
2Quiz becomes availableThe module knowledge check is presented.
3Quiz attempt is recordedProgress and results are saved to the learner record.
4Continue to next moduleSuccessful completion moves the learner forward.
