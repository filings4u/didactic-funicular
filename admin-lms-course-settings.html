General Information

The core information used to identify and present this course throughout the Learning Center.

Displayed in course listings, learner records, and enrollments.
COURSE
THUMBNAIL
Connect this setting to lms_courses.thumbnail_media_id and lms_media when the course settings data layer is wired.

Publishing & Visibility

Control whether learners can find and access this course.

Publish CourseMake the course available according to its enrollment and access rules.
Show in Course CatalogAllow eligible learners to discover this course while browsing available training.
Archive CourseRemove the course from normal active management without immediately deleting its records.

Enrollment & Access

Define how learners receive access to this course.

Use the option that matches your existing enrollment and order workflow.
Allow Re-enrollmentPermit a learner to receive another enrollment if your enrollment rules support it.
Require Active EnrollmentRestrict learner course access to an active enrollment record.

Completion Requirements

Define what a learner must complete before the course can be considered complete.

Require All Required LessonsCourse completion should depend on the learner completing required lesson content.
Require Quiz CompletionRequire attached quizzes when the course structure includes mandatory quiz activities.
Require Assessment CompletionRequire mandatory assessments and evaluate pass/fail results from assessment attempts.
Only applies where the underlying quiz or assessment rules support a passing score.

Certificate Settings

Configure certificate behavior for learners who complete this course.

Enable Course CertificateAllow a certificate to be issued when completion requirements are satisfied.
This will connect to certificate media through lms_certificates.certificate_media_id where supported by your implementation.

Danger Zone

These actions can affect course availability and existing learner records.

Delete should only be enabled after the Supabase data rules confirm how related sections, lessons, enrollments, progress, attempts, and certificates are handled.
